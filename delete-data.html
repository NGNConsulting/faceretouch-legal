Delete Your Data — Face Retouch: AI Photo Editor

Last updated: November 3, 2026 · Published by NGN Consulting Corp

How to request deletion

You can permanently delete your account and data directly in the app — no waiting, no email required:

  1. Open Face Retouch: AI Photo Editor on your device.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Choose whether to also delete your locally saved style profiles, then confirm Delete everything.

If you no longer have the app installed and still want your data deleted, email faceretouch.support@gmail.com from the email address associated with your request (if any) or describe your device/install so we can locate the record, and we will process the deletion manually.

What gets deleted

  • Your anonymous account record on our server (Supabase) — user ID, account creation timestamp, subscription tier, and daily edit count.
  • Your purchase-record link (RevenueCat) tied to that anonymous ID. (Your actual purchase history remains in your Google Play account, as with any app — that's controlled by Google, not us, and can be reviewed/restored independently of this deletion.)
  • All local app data and settings on your device, including your style profiles (if you chose to include them).

What is never collected in the first place

Your photos and face data never leave your device and are never stored on any server, so there is nothing of that kind to delete. Face positions used during an edit are destroyed as soon as the edit finishes (see the Privacy Policy).

Retention period

Deletion is immediate — there is no additional retention period. The server-side deletion happens synchronously when you confirm in the app; nothing is queued for later removal.
