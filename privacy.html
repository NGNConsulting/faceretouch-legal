Face Retouch: AI Photo Editor — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 27, 2026

Zero data collection

Face Retouch: AI Photo Editor collects zero data types and shares zero data types with third parties. We do not use analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, or trackers of any kind.

On-device processing

All face detection, style analysis, and photo enhancement run entirely on your device using on-device AI models. Your photos and any biometric information derived from them never leave your phone and are never uploaded to any server.

What Supabase stores

For anonymous sign-in and subscription state we store only: an anonymous user ID, the account creation timestamp, your subscription tier, and a daily edit count. No photos, no biometric data, no style profiles.

What RevenueCat stores

Purchase records only (which plan you bought and its status), tied to your anonymous user ID for restoring purchases. No personal or photo data.

What our natural-language editing proxy receives

When you use natural-language editing, our server-side proxy receives text only — your written instruction plus your current style settings (numbers). No images, no biometric data, no device identifiers are sent, and your instruction text is never logged.

Enhancement pipeline

Enhancement uses on-device AI models that run entirely on your device. Zero model-inference data is transmitted anywhere.

Deleting your data

You can delete everything at any time from Settings → Delete account in the app. This removes your anonymous server record and wipes all local app data. Your style profiles live only on your device and are deleted with the rest of your local data.

Contact

Questions about this policy or a data deletion request: faceretouch.support@gmail.com
