Face Retouch: AI Photo Editor — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026 · Published by NGN Consulting Corp

Your photos stay on your phone

Face Retouch: AI Photo Editor never collects, uploads, or shares your photos. All photo editing runs on your device. We do not use analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, or trackers of any kind. A small amount of account metadata is collected for app functionality, described below.

How face editing works

To edit a photo, the App finds each face and the position of a few features (eyes, nose, mouth corners) so it can line the face up for the on-device enhancement model. This runs only on your device, only for the photo you choose, and only while you are working on that photo.

The App does not perform face recognition. It never identifies or verifies anyone, and it never creates, stores, or shares face templates, faceprints, or any other biometric identifier. We (NGN Consulting Corp) never receive any face data.

Face data retention and destruction policy

Face positions exist only in your device's memory while you work on a photo, so the sliders can update the preview. They are permanently destroyed when you pick another photo, leave the editor, or close the App, whichever comes first. They are never written to storage, uploaded, sold, leased, traded, or otherwise disclosed, and never used for any purpose other than the edit you requested.

Where edited photos are kept

Edited photos go to your camera roll only when you choose to save them. So you can see recent work on the Home screen, the App keeps your last 12 edits in its private temporary storage on your device. Other temporary copies, including the copy of a photo you pick, are cleared the next time the App starts. Delete account removes all of them.

Your consent

Before your first edit, the App explains this on-device face processing and asks for your agreement. Only the date you agreed is stored, on your device. If you do not agree, editing stays off. You can withdraw your agreement at any time with Settings → Delete account, which also wipes all local app data.

The same processing applies to everyone in a photo you edit, such as a group photo, and nothing about them leaves your device either. Please only edit photos of other people with their permission.

Reference photos

Reference photos you pick for a style profile are not analyzed and never uploaded. Temporary copies are cleared the next time the App starts.

What Supabase stores

For anonymous sign-in and subscription state we store only: an anonymous user ID, the account creation timestamp, and a subscription tier field. No photos, no face data, no style profiles. Your daily edit count stays on your device.

What RevenueCat stores

Purchase records only (which plan you bought and its status), tied to your anonymous user ID for restoring purchases. No personal or photo data.

What happens when you use Describe your edit

When you use Describe your edit, our server-side proxy receives text only: your written instruction plus your current style settings (numbers) and a sign-in token to verify your account. It forwards your instruction and style numbers to OpenAI, which works out the adjustment. No images, no face data, no device identifiers are sent. We do not log your instruction, and we turn off OpenAI's response storage. OpenAI may retain API requests for up to 30 days to monitor abuse, as its API terms allow.

Enhancement pipeline

Enhancement uses face-detection, face-enhancement, upscaling, and hair-segmentation models that run entirely on your device. None of them performs face recognition. Zero model-inference data is transmitted anywhere.

Deleting your data

You can delete everything at any time from Settings → Delete account in the App. This removes your anonymous server record and purchase record and wipes all local app data, including recent edits, temporary photo copies, and the downloaded AI models. Your style profiles live only on your device and are deleted with the rest of your local data. Purchases stay with your Google account and can be restored. See also Delete your data.
